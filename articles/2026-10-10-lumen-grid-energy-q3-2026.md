## Overview

Lumen Grid had a solid third quarter. Revenue rose 10.8% to $1.84 billion, with the renewables business doing most of the work. Profit grew faster than sales, but the company is spending heavily on grid upgrades, so only part of the extra cash reaches shareholders.

## Profitability

Operating income rose 15.8% to $345 million, lifting the margin from 18.0% to 18.8%. The shift toward renewables, which earn higher margins than the services work being wound down, explains most of the gain.

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating income | $298M | $345M | +15.8% |
| Operating margin | 18.0% | 18.8% | +0.8 pts |
| Net income | $161M | $182M | +13.0% |

## Cash flow

Operating cash flow was $640 million. Capital spending climbed 13.9% to $410 million as the company rebuilt transmission lines and added solar capacity, leaving free cash flow of $230 million, up 9.5%.

| Metric | Q3 2025 | Q3 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Operating cash flow | $570M | $640M | +12.3% |
| Capital spending | $360M | $410M | +13.9% |
| Free cash flow | $210M | $230M | +9.5% |

## Takeaways

- The mix is improving: more of the profit now comes from higher-margin renewables.
- Capital spending is growing nearly as fast as cash flow, so free cash flow improves only slowly.
- Exiting low-margin services work shrinks revenue in that segment but helps profitability.
- Next quarter: whether solar additions keep renewables growth above 30%.
