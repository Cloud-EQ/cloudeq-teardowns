## Overview

Cummins India Ltd is a part of the US$23.6 Bn Cummins Inc., which held a 51% stake as of June 2026, and benefits from its parent’s technology, advanced engineering, and R&D capabilities to develop fuel-efficient, emission-compliant products across multiple energy sources.

The company designs, makes, distributes and services diesel and alternative-fuel engines (2.8 to 100 litres), generator sets of up to 3,000 kW (3,750 kVA) and related components. In FY26, 83% of sales came from India and 17% from exports.

The Financial Headline: Trailing Twelve-Month (TTM) revenue of ₹12,662 crore (+16.87% year on year) and net income of ₹2,367 crore, operating margin of about 20%. Free cash flow for FY26 was ₹1,487 crore.

### Business Segments

- **Engines:** the core power solutions business, covering power generation, aftermarket and exports.
- **Lubes:** run through Valvoline Cummins, a 50:50 joint venture with Valvoline International Inc. of the USA, which makes and sells lubricants, greases and allied products under the Valvoline brand.

### Business divisions

- **Industrial:** engines and power systems for several end markets.
  - Railways: propulsion systems, diesel alternator sets, track maintenance machine engines and hotel load converters for Indian Railways.
  - Marine: engines and generator sets for defence and commercial vessels, including submarines.
  - Mining: engines for dump trucks, excavators, dozers and underground equipment, with larger (190-tonne plus) dump truck solutions in development.
  - Defence: engines for wheeled and tracked platforms, plus work on towed artillery and light tank solutions.
  - Pumps, oil and gas, and construction: high-horsepower pump engines, gas compression engines, and excavator engines.
- **Power generation:** diesel and alternative-fuel gensets and integrated power solutions for commercial, industrial, infrastructure, residential and data centre customers. The company launched an 82.5 kVA genset on the X3.7 engine, offers CAQM- and CPCB IV+ compliant gensets, and is developing solutions for data centres, where reliability and uptime matter most. It delivered more than 26,000 gensets in India in FY26.
- **Export:** high-horsepower power generation products and low-horsepower "fit-for-market" solutions sold in Africa, the Middle East, Europe, Latin America, North and Central America, and Asia Pacific. Recent launches include a 500 kVA emissionized genset for Chile and uprated 38L and 50L electronic engines for North and Central America.
- **Engine:** diesel, natural gas and alternative-fuel engines for industrial, commercial and other uses.
- **Distribution:** aftermarket parts, service and support through one parts distribution centre, more than 480 customer touchpoints and more than 110 retail touchpoints. Recent initiatives include engine repowering for Railways, Defence and Power Generation, dual-fuel kits for mining, a battery energy storage product (BESS), the DATUM S fuel tracking and IoT tool, and the Cummins Care App.

Management expects the distribution business to have the potential to grow at around 20% a year over FY27 and the years after. Aftermarket work is tied to engines already in the field, which usually makes it steadier than new equipment sales.

## Future Growth

Cummins India Ltd. is part of the Cummins Inc. group, a global power technology company valued at **US$23.6 billion**, which held a **51% stake as of June 2026**. Through this relationship, Cummins India benefits from its parent’s global engineering expertise, advanced technologies, and R&D capabilities to develop fuel-efficient, emissions-compliant products across conventional and emerging energy technologies, supporting long-term growth and the transition towards more sustainable power solutions.

### Technology Sharing and Global Portfolio

Cummins Inc. operates across four major product families:

- **Accelera:** Zero-emission technologies and solutions.
- **Components:** Engine components, emission solutions, automated transmissions, and drivetrain and braking systems.
- **Engines:** Diesel, natural gas, and alternative-fuel engines.
- **Generators and Power Systems:** Integrated power generation systems and related solutions.

These product families represent the parent company's global portfolio rather than a direct breakdown of products manufactured or sold by Cummins India. However, the group's engineering capabilities and technology-sharing opportunities can help the Indian business adapt to evolving customer requirements and emissions standards.

### Sustainability and the Transition to Cleaner Energy

Through **Accelera**, Cummins Inc. is expanding beyond conventional engine technologies into solutions designed to support the energy transition. Its portfolio includes:

- **Battery systems:** Store electrical energy for use in vehicles, industrial equipment, and stationary energy storage applications.
- **Electric motors:** for vehicles, machinery, and industrial equipment.
- **ePowertrain systems:** Integrate electric motors and associated powertrain technologies to enable electric propulsion.
- **Electrolyzers:** Use electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen. When powered by renewable electricity, they can produce green hydrogen, offering potential for lower-emission industrial processes and energy applications.

Together, these technologies position Cummins Inc. to address growing demand for lower-emission power and propulsion solutions while continuing to serve markets that depend on conventional engines and generators. Improvements in battery technology, electric powertrains, and hydrogen production could create additional opportunities as infrastructure develops and costs become more competitive.

For Cummins India, the long-term opportunity lies in leveraging the parent group's technology and R&D capabilities to respond to India's evolving energy and industrial needs.

## Takeaways

- **Sustained revenue growth:** Revenue increased from ₹6,171 crore in FY22 to ₹12,143 crore in FY26, nearly doubling over four years. Growth has remained strong, supported by demand across power generation, industrial applications, and exports.

- **Profitability has improved:** Net income rose from ₹934 crore in FY22 to ₹2,362 crore in FY26, while operating margins expanded from approximately 14% to 21%. However, the latest quarterly margin decline suggests that sustaining these profitability levels remains important.

- **Quarterly performance warrants attention:** In Q1 FY27, revenue reached ₹3,426 crore, while operating margin declined to 18.0% from 21.3% in Q1 FY26. Investors should monitor whether this reflects temporary cost pressures or a more sustained moderation in profitability.

- **Strong cash generation supports financial flexibility:** Cash from operations increased to ₹1,734 crore in FY26, while free cash flow reached ₹1,487 crore. However, cash conversion softened, and substantial financing outflows highlight the importance of monitoring dividend payments, capital allocation, and the sustainability of cash generation.

- **Sustainability presents a long-term opportunity:** Cummins Inc.'s Accelera portfolio includes battery systems, electric motors, ePowertrain systems, and electrolyzers. The parent group's technology and R&D capabilities could support Cummins India's longer-term transition towards lower-emission power solutions. However, the Indian company's specific exposure to these technologies and their potential revenue contribution remain to be established.

- **What to watch next:** The key indicators are whether operating margins recover towards 20%, operating cash flow remains aligned with earnings, Lubes continues to gain share, and Cummins India discloses tangible progress in adopting or commercializing lower-emission technologies.
